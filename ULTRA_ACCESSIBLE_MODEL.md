# 🎉 ULTRA-ACCESSIBLE TRIBE MODEL

## 💡 Core Philosophy

**TribeFit is NOT a money business!**

It's about:
- ✅ **Fun accountability** through community
- ✅ **Progressive engagement** that evolves
- ✅ **Cross-tribe collaboration** for social good
- ❌ NOT punishment or fines

## 💰 Ultra-Accessible Pricing

### **Skip Costs (Accountability, Not Punishment):**

| Group Type | Cost | Purpose |
|------------|------|---------|
| **Squad** | 5 TC | Light accountability ($5) |
| **Tribe** | 3 TC | Reward for commitment ($3) |

**Savings:** 2 TC per skip (40% off)

### **Coach Sessions (Tribe-Exclusive):**

| Coach Level | Experience | Price |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| **Beginner** | 30-99 workouts | 10 TC |
| **Intermediate** | 100-249 workouts | 12-15 TC |
| **Advanced** | 250-499 workouts | 16-18 TC |
| **Expert** | 500+ workouts | 20 TC |

**Only tribes can access coaches!**

**Pricing Formula:**
```javascript
coachLevel = Math.min(Math.floor(totalWorkouts / 50), 10);
sessionPrice = 10 + coachLevel; // 10-20 TC
```

```

────────────────────────────────────
Monthly cost: 21 TC ($21)
Monthly savings: 4 TC ($4)
Extra rewards: 2.3 TC ($2.30)
Total value: 6.3 TC ($6.30)
```

**Still very affordable + real benefits!** 🎉

## 🎮 Coach System (Tribe-Exclusive)

### **Requirements to Become a Coach:**
```
✅ Must be in a tribe (not squad)
✅ Minimum 14-day streak
✅ Minimum 30 total workouts
✅ Good standing in community
```

### **Pricing Tiers:**
```
Level 0: 10 TC (30-49 workouts)
Level 1: 11 TC (50-99 workouts)
Level 2: 12 TC (100-149 workouts)
Level 3: 13 TC (150-199 workouts)
Level 4: 14 TC (200-249 workouts)
Level 5: 15 TC (250-299 workouts)
Level 6: 16 TC (300-349 workouts)
Level 7: 17 TC (350-399 workouts)
Level 8: 18 TC (400-449 workouts)
Level 9: 19 TC (450-499 workouts)
Level 10: 20 TC (500+ workouts)
```

**Progression-based pricing!** 🎓

### **Why Tribe-Only?**
1. **Quality Control** - Tribes have proven commitment
2. **Community Trust** - Tribes have governance
3. **Sustainability** - Higher engagement = better coaches
4. **Exclusivity** - Incentive to upgrade to tribe

## 🌟 Community Events (Tribe-Exclusive)

### **1. Tribe vs Tribe Competitions:**
```
Entry: 5-10 TC per person
Format: Fitness challenges, streak battles
Prizes: Winner tribe gets 50% of pool to vault
Focus: Bragging rights > money
```

**Example:**
```
2 tribes, 10 members each
Entry: 5 TC per person
Total pool: 100 TC
Winner: 50 TC to tribe vault
Runners-up: 30 TC to vault
Participation: 20 TC split among all
```

### **2. Community Charity Events:**
```
Donation: 10 TC per participant
Format: Workout challenges for charity
Impact: Real donations to local gyms/causes
Recognition: Donor badges, leaderboards
```

**Example:**
```
Tribe with 12 members
Each donates: 10 TC
Total: 120 TC to charity
Platform matches: 20% = 24 TC
Total impact: 144 TC ($144 to charity!)
```

### **3. Inter-Tribe Social Good Projects:**
```
Entry: 10-20 TC per tribe
Format: Multi-tribe collaboration
Goals: Community gym equipment, public fitness spaces
Impact: Lasting community benefit
```

**Example:**
```
5 tribes collaborate
Each tribe: 50 TC (5 members × 10 TC)
Total pool: 250 TC
Project: Outdoor fitness equipment for public park
Community impact: Everyone benefits!
```

### **4. Seasonal Challenges:**
```
Entry: Free or 5 TC
Duration: 30-90 days
Rewards: Badges, leaderboard status
Community: Multiple tribes compete together
```
