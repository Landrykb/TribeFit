# 🇯🇵 YEN-BASED PRICING MODEL

## 💴 Core Economics

**1 TC = 100 yen (¥100)**

This makes TribeFit **ultra-accessible** and easy to understand globally.

---

## 💰 Skip Costs

| Group Type | Cost (TC) | Cost (Yen) | Purpose |
|------------|-----------|------------|---------|
| **Squad** | 2 TC | ¥200 | Light accountability |
| **Tribe** | 1 TC | ¥100 | Rewarded commitment |

**Tribe Savings:** 1 TC (¥100) per skip = **50% discount**

### **Why This Works:**
- ¥100 = Cost of a drink from a vending machine
- ¥200 = Cost of a coffee at a café
- **Not punishment** - just a gentle nudge
- **Extremely affordable** - anyone can participate

```javascript
coachLevel = Math.min(Math.floor(totalWorkouts / 50), 10);
```

4. **Better Experience** - Engaged communities make better coaches

## 🌟 Community Events (Tribe-Exclusive)

### **1. Tribe vs Tribe Competitions:**
- **Entry:** 5-10 TC (¥500-1,000) per person
- **Format:** Fitness challenges, streak battles, workout competitions
- **Prizes:** Winner tribe gets vault bonus + bragging rights
- **Focus:** Community engagement > money

**Example:**
```
2 tribes compete, 10 members each
Entry: 5 TC (¥500) per person
Total pool: 100 TC (¥10,000)
Winner: 50 TC (¥5,000) to vault
Runner-up: 30 TC (¥3,000) to vault
All participants: Recognition badges
```

### **2. Community Charity Events:**
- **Donation:** 10 TC (¥1,000) per participant
- **Format:** Workout challenges for real-world charity
- **Impact:** Actual donations to local gyms, fitness programs, community centers
- **Recognition:** Donor badges, leaderboards, social good impact

**Example:**
```
Tribe with 15 members
Each donates: 10 TC (¥1,000)
Total: 150 TC (¥15,000)
Platform matches 20%: 30 TC (¥3,000)
Total charity impact: ¥18,000 to local cause!
```

### **3. Inter-Tribe Social Good Projects:**
- **Contribution:** 10-20 TC (¥1,000-2,000) per tribe
- **Format:** Multiple tribes collaborate on shared goals
- **Projects:** Outdoor fitness equipment, community gym improvements
- **Legacy:** Lasting community benefit

**Example:**
```
5 tribes collaborate
Each tribe contributes: 50 TC (¥5,000)
  └─ 10 members × 5 TC each
Total pool: 250 TC (¥25,000)
Project: Public park outdoor gym equipment
Community: Everyone benefits!
```

### **4. Seasonal Challenges:**
- **Entry:** Free or 5 TC (¥500)
- **Duration:** 30-90 days
- **Rewards:** Badges, exclusive achievements, leaderboard status
- **Community:** Cross-tribe participation, global engagement

## 📊 Monthly Economics

```

────────────────────────────────────

```

**¥400/month = Less than a movie ticket!** 🎬

### **Tribe Member (Typical Month):**
```

Skip cost: 2 × 1 TC = 2 TC (¥200) [save 2 TC!]

Vault bonus: 2 TC × 15% = 0.3 TC (¥30) [free!]
Streak multiplier: 10 TC × 1.2 = 2 TC (¥200) [extra!]
────────────────────────────────────
Monthly spend: 22 TC (¥2,200)
Savings: 2 TC (¥200) on skips
Extra rewards: 2.3 TC (¥230)
Total value: 4.3 TC (¥430) in benefits
Net cost: ~17.7 TC (¥1,770)
```

**¥1,770/month = Less than a single personal training session!** 💪

## 💡 Design Philosophy

### **Why Yen-Based?**

1. **🇯🇵 Japanese Market Focus**
   - Natural fit for Japanese users
   - Easy mental math (¥100 increments)
   - Familiar pricing structure

2. **🌍 Global Accessibility**
   - Still works everywhere
   - Simple conversion (1 TC = ¥100)
   - Transparent pricing

3. **💰 Ultra-Affordable**
   - ¥100 skip = vending machine drink
   - ¥1,500 coach = less than restaurant meal
   - ¥2,000/month = extremely accessible

4. **🎮 Fun, Not Punishment**
   - Small amounts encourage participation
   - Community accountability > financial pain
   - Focus on building habits, not extracting money

### **Not a Money Business:**
```
✅ Building community
✅ Encouraging fitness habits
✅ Social good projects
✅ Cross-tribe collaboration
✅ Long-term engagement

❌ NOT extracting revenue
❌ NOT punishing financially
❌ NOT expensive barriers
```
