# 🎉 Fun-Focused Economics - REVISED

## 💡 Philosophy Change

### ❌ Old Approach (Too Punitive):
- Skip: 100 TC → 80 TC ($100 → $80) 
- Coach: 150 TC → 135 TC ($150 → $135)
- **Problem:** Feels like punishment, not fun!

### ✅ New Approach (Fun & Community):
- Skip: 25 TC → 20 TC ($25 → $20)
- Coach: 50 TC → 40 TC ($50 → $40)
- **Goal:** Accountability through community, not financial pain

### **Squad Member (10 workouts/month):**
```

Coach session: 1 × 50 TC = 50 TC
────────────────────────────────────
Monthly spend: ~100 TC ($100)
```
**Affordable, not punitive!** ✅

### **Tribe Member (10 workouts/month):**
```
Skip 2 workouts: 2 × 20 TC = 40 TC (save 10 TC)
Coach session: 1 × 40 TC = 40 TC (save 10 TC)
Vault bonus: 10 TC × 15% = 1.5 TC (free!)
Streak multiplier: 10 TC × 1.2 = 2 TC extra
────────────────────────────────────
Monthly spend: 80 TC ($80)
Monthly savings: 20 TC ($20)
Extra rewards: 3.5 TC ($3.50)
Total value: 23.5 TC ($23.50)
```
**Real community benefit!** 🎉

## 💰 Vault Economics (Fun Growth)

### **Squad Vault:**
```
Member skips: 25 TC
└─ 20% to vault = 5 TC
└─ 80% to members = 20 TC (4 members × 5 TC)

After 10 skips:
- Vault: 50 TC
- Members earned: 200 TC total
- Community benefit ✓
```

### **Tribe Vault (with 15% bonus):**
```
Member skips: 20 TC
└─ 20% to vault = 4 TC
└─ 15% bonus = 0.6 TC
└─ Total vault = 4.6 TC
└─ 80% to members = 16 TC

After 10 skips:
- Vault: 46 TC (vs 50 for squad, but cheaper skips!)
- Members earned: 160 TC total
- Plus 6 TC bonus free
- Total community benefit: 166 TC ✓
```
